# How the sandbox maps onto the US today (survey, 29 Oct 2026)

**Method.** I opened every source cited here on 29 Oct 2026. I also took two measurements of my own, using `curl` with a desktop Chrome user agent from a US IP (Akamai geolocated it to New York). Both measurements read static HTML only: no JavaScript ran and there was one attempt per URL. **[unverified]** marks a claim I could not confirm on a primary page.

## 1. Component map and verdicts

| Sandbox component | Real-world counterparts | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Bank website**: consent overlay, password login, OTP for an unrecognised device, statement date picker, PDF download | Chase says that at a first sign-in, or when activity is unusual, it uses "mobile app notifications or a one-time code" ([Chase](https://www.chase.com/digital/resources/privacy-security/security/how-we-protect-you)). All 14 large US banks and card issuers I looked up in the [2fa.directory dataset](https://api.2fa.directory/v4/all.json) support SMS codes. 10 of 13 big-bank login endpoints set Akamai Bot Manager cookies (`_abck`, `bm_sz`) (own measurement, §3). Consent-manager scripts (OneTrust, Ketch) appeared on only 3 of 13 login pages in static HTML. | **Sandbox easier.** OTP-on-new-device is realistic (realistic condition). The sandbox has no bot detection, device fingerprinting or app push approvals. Real logins add step-up checks driven by risk scoring. The *blocking* consent overlay is probably harsher than typical US practice **[unverified: static HTML cannot show whether a banner blocks the page]**. |
| **Bank bespoke APIs**: a different API per bank; consumer OAuth consent in the realistic condition | Third parties reach banks through aggregators: Plaid, MX, Finicity/Mastercard, Akoya. FDX reports "more than 114 million customer connections" on FDX-aligned APIs and "over 1,000 data providers" ([FDX letter to CFPB, 20 Oct 2025](https://financialdataexchange.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FDX-ANPR-Response-Docket-No-CFPB-2025-0037.pdf)). Plaid OAuth sends the user to the bank to authenticate and authorise sharing; at Chase, for example, accounts are chosen in Chase's own OAuth flow ([Plaid docs](https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/)). Akoya says "No screen scraping" and 4,300+ connected FIs ([Akoya](https://akoya.com/fintechs?hsLang=en)). MX (2022) lists "necessary screen scrapes" among its connection methods ([MX](https://www.mx.com/blog/connect-consumers-to-financial-accounts-safely-securely/)). | **Mixed.** *Access is easier* in the sandbox: no bank issues API credentials to a consumer or a personal agent today. Access requires registering as a data recipient under bilateral agreements, which JPMorgan now charges for (below). *Schemas are harder*: real access is largely standardised (FDX) behind one aggregator API. The sandbox therefore models "each bank exposes its own consumer API", which is hypothetical. |
| **Statements** (2 months × 4 accounts) | Fannie Mae requires, for a purchase, statements covering "the most recent full two-month period". It accepts statements "downloaded by the borrower from the Internet" ([B3-4.2-01](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-4.2-01/verification-deposits-and-assets)). Plaid Statements returns the bank's own PDF; its named banks cover about 40% of US depository accounts, with up to 2 years of history ([Plaid](https://plaid.com/docs/statements/)). | **Similar.** The task matches the GSE requirement exactly. |
| **Payroll site** (hospital "PeoplePay": login, pay-stub list, PDF) and payroll API | Large health systems run enterprise HCM. Workday ranks "#1 ERP for Healthcare" in Best in KLAS 2026 ([Workday datasheet](https://forms.workday.com/content/dam/web/en-us/documents/datasheets/klas-2026-datasheet-enus.pdf)). Employees reach pay stubs in employee self-service portals, often behind employer SSO **[unverified: no hospital-wide share found]**. Consumer-permissioned payroll connectors: Truv claims "96% of the US labor force", using OAuth, SSO, and OTP "credential-less" login for ADP and Paycom ([Truv](https://docs.truv.com/developers/coverage-payroll)). Argyle claims 91% ([Argyle](https://argyle.com/)) and Pinwheel ">80%" ([2022](https://www.pinwheelapi.com/resources/news/employer-expansion)). Plaid Income offers payroll-linked and document-upload income ([Plaid](https://plaid.com/docs/income/)). Lender-side: The Work Number held 209M active records at end-2025 ([Equifax letter](https://assets.equifax.com/assets/corp/2025-Equifax-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)). | **Sandbox easier for the portal** (no employer SSO or MFA). **Harder than many real loans for the borrower**: a lender often verifies employment and income itself (The Work Number, DU validation), so the borrower's agent may never fetch payslips. Two payslips per borrower is plausible: Fannie requires the latest stub within 30 days with YTD earnings ([Selling Guide](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.1-02/standards-employment-documentation)). The sandbox omits W-2s and IRS transcripts. |
| **Lender website**: 6-step application, upload, review, LE after 5 s, one condition, intent to proceed | Point-of-sale portals: Blend is used by "14 of the top 50 US mortgage originators" and processed $1.3T of applications in 2025 ([Blend overview](https://s28.q4cdn.com/402719125/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Blend-Corporate-Overview-2026-Final.pdf)). ICE Encompass also has a consumer portal **[not opened]**. Under Reg Z, the six items in §1026.2(a)(3)(ii) make an application, and the LE is due within 3 business days. A creditor "shall not require" verification documents before the LE (§1026.19(e)(2)(iii), [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/part-1026/section-1026.19)). Intent may be shown "in any manner the consumer chooses", and silence is not intent ([CFPB comment 19(e)(2)(i)(A)-2](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/)). | **Sandbox easier.** Real applications are longer (the full URLA), add identity, credit and e-consent steps, often embed Plaid or Finicity asset linking, and usually raise several conditions. LE timing is compressed from days to seconds. Uploading documents *before* the LE is a lender choice, not a legal requirement. |
| **Lender bespoke API** (B-full) | Lender APIs exist for lenders and approved partners, for example ICE Encompass Developer Connect ([ICE](https://developer.ice.com/mortgage-lending)) and Optimal Blue pricing APIs ([Optimal Blue](https://www2.optimalblue.com/developer)). I found **no public consumer- or agent-facing "apply" API** at any US lender (absence after search; not proof). | **Hypothetical** (easier than reality, which has none). |
| **Common agent API**: grants, server-side checks, records by reference, s2s | Closest pieces: FDX as a common schema with OAuth consent; DU validation, where the lender submits third-party verification reports to Fannie Mae ([B3-2-02](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-2-02/du-validation-service)); IRS IVES, which lets taxpayers authorise a lender "through their online IRS account" ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/income-verification-express-service)); agent-identity schemes such as Cloudflare signed agents ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/signed-agents/)) and Akamai/Visa Trusted Agent Protocol ([Visa](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.21956.html)). | **Hypothetical.** Only fragments exist, and none is cross-sector or consumer-held. The CFPB 1033 rule, the strongest push toward mandated standard access, is enjoined. |
| **Rate discovery**: 3 lender rate pages with JSON-LD `schema:MortgageLoan` plus a rates index | Lenders publish advertised rates on web pages, usually rendered by JavaScript and assuming a ZIP code, credit score and points. Bankrate rate tables are paid advertiser listings ([disclosure](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/mortgage-rates/)). Zillow offers only *average* quoted rates to approved partners ([Zillow API](https://www.zillowgroup.com/developers/api/mortgage/get-current-rates)). LendingTree sells leads, not an open API **[not opened]**. Optimal Blue's PPE and lead-quoting APIs (e.g. for LendingTree and Zillow) are B2B. JSON-LD sample: §2. | **Sandbox easier.** Structured rates are rare (2 of 10 lender pages, one stale) and nobody publishes closing costs in structured form. Advertised rates are not offers; only an LE is. |
| **Session expiry** (friction condition: after N page requests) | NIST SP 800-63B rev. 4: at AAL2, re-authenticate after at most 1 h inactivity and 24 h overall ([NIST](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html)). Example bank: 20 min idle ([MidFirst](https://www.midfirst.com/time-out)). | **Different mechanism.** The baseline has no timeouts (easier). Request-count expiry is harsher than time-based expiry for fast agents and milder for slow ones. |
| **Bot detection / CAPTCHA** | Akamai Bot Manager is on most big-bank logins (§3). Imperva: financial services was the top account-takeover target in 2024 (22% of ATO attacks) ([Imperva](https://www.imperva.com/blog/2025-imperva-bad-bot-report-how-ai-is-supercharging-the-bot-threat/)). | **Sandbox much easier: there is none.** Real cloud-browser agents get blocked. OpenAI says "Some websites block cloud browsers" ([dots docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)). |
| **Agent runtime**: `get_credentials`, human OTP relay, `ask_user` | Meta Muse keeps credentials in a "Secure Credentials Store". Its email connector filters out one-time codes and login links, and the agent is paused while the user has control ([Meta help](https://meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/2124746764949121), [Meta research, 8 Sep 2026](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse)). OpenAI dots asks the user to enter credentials and verification in a private form, or to "Take over" ([docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)). Grok Bot fills secrets by reference without the model seeing them and hands the user the computer for "a CAPTCHA" or a bank card verification ([xAI](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy)). | **Similar in shape.** Real products route OTPs and CAPTCHAs to a human, as the sandbox's OTP relay does. In the sandbox the model sees credentials via `get_credentials`, whereas the products hide them (a security difference, not a cost one). None of the three publishes handback rates. |

## 2. Own measurement: structured rate data on lender pages (29 Oct 2026, ~15:42 GMT)

I fetched the main rate page of 12 lenders and 2 marketplaces and parsed `application/ld+json` blocks and microdata (script in scratchpad, not shipped).

- **Fetched:** 10 lenders. Rocket Mortgage returned **403** from AkamaiGHost ("Unable to Process Request"). PNC reset the HTTP/2 stream.
- **Lenders with schema.org rate data: 2 of 10.**
  - **Chase**: one `FinancialProduct` with `interestRate` 6.875 and APR 7.079, `dateModified` today and `areaServed` IL (Chicago). Its `MortgageLoan` node carries no rate.
  - **Better**: one `MortgageLoan` with `interestRate` "2.500", while the visible page shows 5.250%. It is **stale**.
- **No structured rate data: 8 of 10.**
  - Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, Citi, loanDepot, Rate.com and Navy Federal had none. BofA and Truist had only FAQ, organisation or site-level types.
  - In 7 of 10 lender pages no rate-like percentage appeared in static HTML at all; rates are loaded by script.
- **Marketplaces:**
  - Zillow: 18 `MortgageLoan` nodes with rate, APR, points and fees as free text, and a 24 h `validThrough`.
  - Bankrate: a `Dataset` naming `variableMeasured` but carrying no values.
- **Closing costs:** no page published them in structured form. The sandbox's lenders publish rate, APR, points, example payment and closing costs.

## 4. Numbers worth quoting

1. **>114M** customer connections on FDX-aligned APIs; **>1,000** data providers; token-based APIs are now "about two-thirds" of user-permissioned traffic. Source: FDX comment to CFPB, dated 20 Oct 2025 ([PDF](https://financialdataexchange.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FDX-ANPR-Response-Docket-No-CFPB-2025-0037.pdf)). The remaining third (about) is still credential-based.
2. **CFPB 1033:** finalised Oct 2024; preliminary injunction **29 Oct 2025**, E.D. Ky., *Forcht Bank v. CFPB* ([ABA Banking Journal, 3 Nov 2025](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2025/11/kentucky-federal-court-enjoins-cfpb-from-enforcing-current-1033-final-rule/)); still "effectively unenforceable" on 26 Jun 2026 ([Ballard Spahr](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/06/26/open-banking-regulation-in-2026-federal-regulation-resurfaces-as-states-bring-data-sharing-into-focus/)); reconsideration proposal at OIRA by 6 Aug 2026 ([Ballard Spahr](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/08/06/cfpb-sends-new-section-1033-open-banking-proposal-to-oira-for-review/)). **[unverified]** whether OIRA review has concluded or a proposal has been published by 29 Oct 2026; reginfo.gov was down when I tried.
3. **JPMorgan** reached paid data-access agreements with Plaid, Yodlee, Morningstar and Akoya ([The Paypers, 17 Nov 2025](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/jpmorgan-chase-reaches-paid-data-access-agreements-with-aggregators)).
4. **The Work Number: 209M active** and 813M total employment records at end-2025 ([Equifax 2025 shareholder letter](https://assets.equifax.com/assets/corp/2025-Equifax-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)).
5. **Fannie Mae**: purchase loans need "the most recent full two-month period" of statements (B3-4.2-01). The latest paystub must be dated within 30 days of application (B3-3.2-01). Guide published 7 Oct 2026.
6. **Reg Z**:
   - The LE is due within **3 business days** of an application.
   - The lender cannot require verification documents before the LE.
   - The consumer may indicate intent "in any manner"; silence is not intent.
7. **Blend**: 14 of the top 50 originators; $1.3T of applications processed in 2025 (Blend corporate overview 2026).
8. **Structured rates: 2 of 10** major lender rate pages carry schema.org rate data, and one of those is stale. **None of the 14 pages sampled** (12 lenders, 2 marketplaces) put closing costs in structured form. Own measurement, 29 Oct 2026.
9. **10 of 13** big-bank login endpoints run Akamai Bot Manager. Rocket's rate page returned 403 to a scripted client. Own measurement, 29 Oct 2026.
10. Financial services took **22%** of account-takeover attacks in 2024; bots were **51%** of web traffic ([Imperva, 15 Apr 2025](https://www.imperva.com/blog/2025-imperva-bad-bot-report-how-ai-is-supercharging-the-bot-threat/)).
11. **Handback data:** Muse, dots and Grok Bot publish none (checked docs). The nearest published figure is internal Claude Code use, where human interventions per session fell from **5.4 to 3.3** between Aug and Dec 2025 ([Anthropic, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy)). That figure is for coding, not web tasks.

## 6. Suggested wording for the paper

The documents requested match GSE rules, and the OTP, OAuth-consent and human-handback patterns match current bank practice and agent products. The browser arm is optimistic: there is no bot management, no employer SSO, the application is short with one condition, and the rate pages are cleaner than real ones. The per-organization consumer APIs and the common agent API are hypothetical. Today data reaches third parties through contracted aggregators, mostly over FDX-aligned APIs, and the common agent API has only partial analogues (FDX, DU validation, IVES consent, signed agents).
